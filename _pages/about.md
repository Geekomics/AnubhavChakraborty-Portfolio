Molecular biologist and genetic engineer with a PhD in Cell Biology and Physiology (awarded August 2026) and 7 years of research experience specializing in functional genomics, target discovery, CRISPR-based genome engineering, epigenetic modulation, and viral/non-viral gene delivery platforms for kidney diseases.

***

### Research Highlights & Platform Capabilities

* **CRISPRa Therapeutics:** Engineered a targeted CRISPR activation gene therapy augmenting endogenous *PKD1* expression to rescue disease phenotypes in Autosomal Dominant Polycystic Kidney Disease (ADPKD).
  * **Pending Patent:** *CRISPRa Compositions, Methods of Use Thereof for Treating Polycystic Kidney Disease, and Methods of Making the Same* (U.S. Provisional Patent Application No. 64/034,047).
* **Plasmid Engineering Platform:** Developed an innovative vector miniaturization process achieving a ~3x enhancement in transgene delivery and expression in hard-to-transfect primary renal epithelial cells[cite: 5].
* **Vector Delivery Platforms:** Hands-on expertise designing, optimizing, and producing recombinant AAV (rAAV), Lentivirus, Virus-Like Particles (VLPs), Lipid Nanoparticles (LNPs), and split-intein protein trans-splicing systems[cite: 5].

***

### Key Scientific Capabilities

* **Gene Delivery & Engineering:** rAAV, Lentivirus, VLPs, LNPs, Nucleofection, Mini-circle production, Split-intein systems[cite: 5].
* **Functional Genomics & Epigenetics:** CRISPRa/CRISPRi, CRISPR-Cas9/Cas12a editing, Chromatin accessibility assays, Advanced molecular cloning, Stable cell line generation[cite: 5].
* **Disease Models & Cell Culture:** Primary mouse & human renal epithelial cell isolation, 3D cell culture, Mouse surgical models of ischemia-reperfusion injury (IRI), Micro-dissection[cite: 5].
* **Digital & In Silico Tools:** Benchling, SnapGene, LabArchive (ELN), GraphPad Prism, R/Bioconductor[cite: 5].
