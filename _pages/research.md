### The Jared Grantham Kidney Institute | University of Kansas Medical Center
**Predoctoral Candidate** | *Lab of Dr. Alan S. Yu* — Kansas City, KS, USA *(Aug 2020 – Aug 2026)*

* **Engineered Targeted CRISPRa Therapeutics:** Developed a novel CRISPR activation (CRISPRa) gene therapy strategy augmenting endogenous *PKD1* transcription, rescuing disease-associated cellular phenotypes and resulting in a pending U.S. Provisional Patent (No. 64/034,047) and bioRxiv preprint.
* **Overcame Epigenetic Barriers:** Profiling chromatin accessibility and heterochromatin limitations at the proximal promoter of *PKD1*, designing strategies to bypass epigenetic silencing, benchmarked various CRISPR-effectors for optimal epigenomic modulation.
* **Optimized Multi-Vector Delivery Platforms:** Designed, evaluated, and produced rAAVs, Lentiviruses, Virus-Like Particles (VLPs), Lipid Nanoparticles (LNPs), and Nucleofection systems to deliver complex genetic payloads across primary and immortalized cell lines.
* **Developed Vector Miniaturization:** Engineered a plasmid miniaturization strategy that enhanced transgene delivery and expression by **~3-fold** in hard-to-transfect primary renal epithelial cells.
* **Developed Disease Models & Assays:** Designed reporter-based gRNA screening platforms, split-intein protein trans-splicing mechanisms, and primary 3D cell culture models to investigate gene regulation, protein expression, cellular signaling, and disease-associated phenotypes.
* **Provided Cross-Functional Consulting & Leadership:** Advised inter-departmental research groups on custom viral vector design and stable cell line derivation; directly mentored 4 junior researchers (1 intern, 3 pre-med students) in molecular workflows.
