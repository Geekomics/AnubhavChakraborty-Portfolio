### The Jared Grantham Kidney Institute | University of Kansas Medical Center
**PhD Researcher** | *Lab of Dr. Alan S. Yu* | Kansas City, KS, USA *(Aug 2020 – Aug 2026)*

* **Developed a CRISPRa strategy for ADPKD:** Designed and tested CRISPR activation of endogenous *PKD1*, which raised polycystin-1 levels and altered cellular features of disease. This work led to a proof-of-concept for a gene therapy for ADPKD, a U.S. provisional patent application, and peer-reviewed publications.
* **Tackled epigenetic barriers:** Investigated chromatin accessibility and heterochromatin at the PKD1 promoter by ATAC-qPCR and benchmarked dCas9 effectors such as p300, Tet1, VPR, SAM, VP64 and endogenous transcription factors. Screened guide RNAs in an arrayed, reporter-based format and identified the optimal sgRNA for CRISPRa of endogenous PKD1.
* **Explored pharmacologic approaches:** Tested trichostatin and retinoic acid as alternative pharmacologic and gene-regulation approaches to increase *PKD1*, both on their own and in combination with CRISPRa.
* **Engineered and tested 50+ constructs:** Built and verified reporter and expression constructs through multiple cloning strategies including multi-fragment cloning via Gibson and Golden gate Assembly, and compared polycystin-1 expression from different mRNA and cDNA designs to see how payload design shapes protein output.
* **Turned a failed method into a better one:** After poor VSV-G lentiviral transduction in mouse renal epithelial cells, used the results to develop a non-viral approach that improved transgene delivery.
* **Solved a delivery bottleneck in vitro:** Developed a one-pot Type IIS restriction/ligation strategy that reduced a CRISPRa plasmid from about 14.9 kb to 6 kb and improved transgene delivery roughly 3-fold in difficult-to-transfect primary renal epithelial cells.
* **Benchmarked delivery platforms:** Evaluated and optimized nucleofection, lipid-based transfection, VLPs, lentivirus, and rAAV for delivering Cas9/Cas12a RNP, sgRNA, mRNA, and plasmid payloads into primary and immortalized human and mouse renal epithelial cells.
* **Prepared RNP components in-house:** Synthesized sgRNAs by in vitro transcription for Cas9 RNP synthesis.
* **Built disease models and quantitative assays:** Established primary human and mouse renal epithelial cultures (including patient-derived cells), 3D Matrigel microcyst assays, and hanging-drop spheroids. Developed fluorescent reporters, ELISA, flow cytometry, viability, cytotoxicity, mitochondrial membrane potential, and ER stress readouts.
* **Tested drug responses in cells:** Ran dose-response and EC50 experiments with doxycycline to tune inducible gene expression systems, trichostatin to probe epigenetic changes, and antibiotics to set kill curves and measure cellular toxicity.
* **Characterized protein output:** Analyzed polycystin-1 maturation and glycosylation by Western blotting and Endo H/PNGase F assays.
* **Kept results reproducible in variable cells:** Used matched controls and replicates and validated findings across independent cell lines, donors, and experiments, including in patient-derived primary cells.
* **Contributed to collaborative studies:** Helped investigate inflammatory signaling, extracellular matrix remodeling, and tissue remodeling in early PKD, including bulk RNA-seq analysis.
* **Advised and mentored:** Served as a go-to resource for other labs on bespoke viral vector design, plasmid engineering, and stable cell line derivation using transwell feeder-layer and co-culture systems, and trained 4 junior researchers
